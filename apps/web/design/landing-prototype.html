    How it works

    The maths and the meaning
    are kept apart

    Most AI astrology asks one model to do both, and a model will happily invent a position that sounds right. Here it never gets the chance.

    गणित
    Gaṇita — the reckoning

    The ephemeris calculates

    Your birth moment becomes universal time using the zone your birthplace kept that year. Swiss Ephemeris gives every longitude. Lahiri ayanamsa, whole-sign houses, mean nodes. The same inputs always give the same chart.

    फलित
    Phalita — the reading

    The astrologer reads it

    The finished chart is handed over as data. It interprets, compares and explains — but is never asked to work out a degree or a date, so it cannot get one wrong.

      Why charts disagree

      Kathmandu has not
      always been +5:45

      Today’s offset
      +05:45
      Actual, in 1975
      +05:30
      Δ ascendant
      3.75°

      Nepal kept +5:30 until 1986, and local mean time of +5:41:16 before that. A 1975 birth calculated with today’s offset lands fifteen minutes off — roughly 3.75° of ascendant, which is enough to move a lagna into the wrong sign and quietly invalidate everything read from it.

      So we store the zone by name and look up what it meant on your date, rather than storing a number that was only true this decade. A small thing that decides whether the rest of the chart is worth reading.
